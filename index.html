How DNS Bench Pro works

DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH)

Instead of classic DNS on port 53, this tool sends encrypted DNS queries over HTTPS to each resolver's DoH endpoint, from your browser, on your network.

Warm-up

Before timing starts, we send untimed probes so TCP/TLS handshakes don't inflate the first real measurements.

Cached vs uncached

The first query for each domain uses a random subdomain (cold / uncached path). Later queries hit the same domain and usually show faster, cache-warmed responses.

Privacy

There is no server-side logging from this site. Queries go from your browser to the DNS providers you choose. Results stay on your device unless you export or share them.
